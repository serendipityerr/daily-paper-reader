## 摘要
对于$d\in\{1,2\}$，设$H=-\frac{1}{2}Δ+ V +ξ$是$L^2(\mathbb{R}^d)$上的随机Schrödinger算子，其中$ξ$是标准高斯白噪声，$V$是无穷远处具有幂律增长的确定势。利用Schrödinger半群迹的Feynman-Kac公式，通过布朗桥局部时估计，给出了$\mathrm{Tr}[e^{-sH}]$与$\mathrm{Tr}[e^{-tH}]$在$s,t\to0$时协方差的渐近最优上下界。这些估计显著改进了$d=1$情形下的已有结果，并且是$d=2$情形下首次出现的此类估计。作为这些新估计的应用，我们证明了当$s,t\to0$时迹的定量超均匀性质及去相关速率。

## Abstract
For $d\in\{1,2\}$, let $H=-\frac{1}{2}Δ+ V +ξ$ be the random Schrödinger operator on $L^2(\mathbb{R}^d)$ where $ξ$ is a standard Gaussian white noise and $V$ is a deterministic potential with power-law growth at infinity. Using a Feynman-Kac formula for the trace of the Schrödinger semigroup, we give optimal asymptotic upper and lower bounds on the covariance of $\mathrm{Tr}[e^{-sH}]$ and $\mathrm{Tr}[e^{-tH}]$ as $s,t\to0$ through estimates on Brownian bridge local times. These estimates are a significant improvement on previous bounds in the case $d=1$ and are the first of their kind for $d=2$. As an application of these new estimates, we prove a quantitative hyperuniformity-type property and decorrelation rate for the trace as $s,t\to0$.
