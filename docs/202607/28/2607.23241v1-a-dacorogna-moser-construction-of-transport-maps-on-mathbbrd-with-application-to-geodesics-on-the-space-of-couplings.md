## 摘要
Dacorogna和Moser的开创性工作提出了一种在有界域上从一个概率分布到另一个概率分布构造正则传输映射的方法。在本工作中，我们将此构造推广到整个$\mathbb{R}^d$，适用于严格渐近对数凹测度，这是一类包含对数凹测度的Lipschitz扰动的广泛分布。随后，我们利用这一构造研究乘积集上具有给定边际律（耦合）的概率测度空间中的测地线，并推导出最优性条件，从而回答了Conforti、Lacker和Pal近期工作中的开放问题。受Brenier关于不可压缩流体的变分模型及其正则化的启发，我们进一步引入了测地线问题的熵正则化，这可以视为耦合空间上的薛定谔桥问题，并同样推导了最优性条件。最终，我们研究了当正则化消失时极小元的收敛性，并证明了相关拉格朗日乘子的收敛性。我们的方法涉及利用反射耦合的概率概念，通过渐近对数凹测度的结构，证明$\mathbb{R}^d$上椭圆和抛物方程的全局正则性估计在时间上的一致有界性。

## Abstract
A seminal work by Dacorogna and Moser introduced a way of constructing regular transport maps from a probability distribution on a bounded domain to another one. In this work, we extend this construction to the whole $\mathbb{R}^d$ for strictly asymptotically log-concave measures, a wide class of distributions that encompasses Lipschitz-perturbations of log-concave measures. We then leverage this construction to study geodesics in the space of probability measures on a product set with imposed marginal laws (couplings), for which we derive optimality conditions, answering an open question in a recent work by Conforti, Lacker and Pal. Taking inspiration from Brenier's variational model for incompressible fluids and its regularization, we further introduce an entropic regularization of the geodesic problem, which can be seen as the Schrödinger bridge problem on the space of couplings, for which we also derive optimality conditions. We eventually study convergence of minimizers as the regularization vanishes, and we prove convergence of the related Lagrange multipliers. Our approach involves proving uniform-in-time global regularity estimates on elliptic and parabolic equations on $\mathbb{R}^d$, by exploiting the structure of asymptotically log-concave measures using the probabilistic notion of reflection coupling.
