## 摘要
我们解决了在流体中定位化学源的问题。基于浓度场与拉格朗日示踪轨迹之间的对偶性，我们将浓度检测解释为连接源与检测点的路径证据。这种在可能发射位置与检测点之间的薛定谔桥公式，利用了被动示踪物的反向传播子，通过朗之万动力学将源定位视为候选发射位置的采样。相关漂移揭示了经典趋化性和“抛射-搜索”作为从单一输运原理中涌现的互补行为。应用于二维湍流中的嗅觉搜索，所提出的回溯框架通过使用单一伽利略不变的学习传播子，在不同风况下优于经典策略。

## Abstract
We address the problem of locating a chemical source in a flow. Based on the duality between the concentration field and Lagrangian tracer trajectories, we interpret concentration detections as evidence of paths connecting the source to the detection points. This Schrödinger bridge formulation between plausible emission positions and detection points leverages the backward propagator of passive tracers to frame source localization as the sampling of candidate emission locations via Langevin dynamics. The associated drift reveals classical chemotaxis and cast-and-surge as complementary behaviors emerging from a single transport-based principle. Applied to olfactory search in two-dimensional turbulence, the proposed backtracking framework outperforms classical strategies across varying wind regimes using a single, Galilean-invariant learned propagator.
