## 摘要
我们研究了L^p正则化最优传输（1<p<∞）和熵正则化最优传输（EOT）的小正则化极限。在源和目标测度的温和假设下，明确确定了精确的一阶（分别地，二阶）渐近性。我们的工作推广了二次和熵正则化的现有结果，并通过p∈(1,2)的自然插值将它们联系起来。我们通过一种新颖的方法统一推导所有这些渐近性，该方法将最优轮廓的局部计算与边际约束的全局强制执行分开：对偶性导致熵的高斯轮廓和L^p正则化的Barenblatt轮廓，而量化构造将这些局部轮廓转化为耦合。

## Abstract
We study the small-regularization limit for $L^p$-regularized optimal transport with $1<p<\infty$ and for entropically regularized optimal transport (EOT). The exact first-order (respectively, second-order) asymptotics are determined explicitly under mild assumptions on the source and target measures. Our work generalizes the existing results for quadratic and entropic regularization, and connects them by a natural interpolation via $p\in(1,2)$. We derive all these asymptotics in a unified manner by a novel approach that separates the local computation of the optimal profile from the global enforcement of the marginal constraints: convex duality leads to Gaussian profiles for entropy and Barenblatt profiles for $L^p$-regularization, while a quantization construction turns these local profiles into couplings.
