## 摘要
我们构造了一个紧致度量空间，其上赋有无原子概率测度以及有界Lipschitz代价，使得熵最优传输的最小化子没有零温弱极限。更精确地说，当ε↓0时P_ε不收敛。在该例子中，每个未正则化的最小化子关于μ⊗μ是奇异的，因此最优面上的熵恒为+∞。我们用以下方式描述聚点集：\[ \operatorname{Clust}(P_\varepsilon)=\{P_w:w\in\mathcal W\}, \] 其中P_w是权重为w的两个零代价图耦合的混合，而\mathcal W\subset[0,1]是一个非退化的紧区间。然后我们计算该区间中的两个显式点w^-<w^+。这表明代价的紧性、无原子性和Lipschitz正则性并不能保证零温收敛。我们还提出了一个关于一般问题的紧性定理。若C∈L^1(μ⊗ν)在Polish空间上连续且下方有界，则零温聚点集是最优面上的非空弱紧连通子集。在证明中，我们应用了Bernton、Ghosal和Nutz的聚点定理以及ε ↦ π_ε的连续性。最后，我们给出了完全收敛和聚点归属的局部及外部一阶准则。我们证明：不收敛是可能的，但仅能通过最优计划的一个连通连续统实现。

## Abstract
We construct a compact metric space with an atomless probability measure and a bounded Lipschitz cost for which the entropic optimal-transport minimisers have no zero-temperature weak limit. More precisely, $P_\varepsilon$ does not converge as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$. In the example, every unregularised minimiser is singular with respect to $μ\otimesμ$, so that the entropy on the optimal face is identically $+\infty$. We describe the cluster set by \[ \operatorname{Clust}(P_\varepsilon)=\{P_w:w\in\mathcal W\}, \] where $P_w$ is the mixture of the two zero-cost graph couplings with weight $w$, and where $\mathcal W\subset[0,1]$ is a non-degenerate compact interval. We then compute two explicit points $w^-<w^+$ in this interval. This shows that compactness, atomlessness, and Lipschitz regularity of the cost do not imply zero-temperature convergence. We also present a compactness theorem for the general problem. If $C\in L^1(μ\otimesν)$ is continuous and bounded from below on Polish spaces, then the zero-temperature cluster set is a nonempty weakly compact connected subset of the optimal face. In the proof, we apply the cluster-point theorem of Bernton, Ghosal, and Nutz and the continuity of $\varepsilon\mapstoπ_\varepsilon$. Finally, we give local and exterior first-order criteria for full convergence and cluster membership. We show that nonconvergence is possible, but only through a connected continuum of optimal plans.
