## 摘要
我们研究条件于保持在固定区间 $[0,a]$ 内的布朗运动和布朗桥过程，关注单个时刻的条件分布。对于在 $[0,a]$ 中的布朗运动，在给定存活至时刻 $t$ 的条件下，我们回顾（并以自包含形式给出）其在 $t$ 时刻位置的条件密度（精确外推）。对于在 $[0,T]$ 上条件于保持在 $[0,a]$ 内的布朗桥，我们将某个内部时刻的概率密度表示为杀死转移密度的归一化乘积（精确插值）。这些密度具有对偶互补级数表示，并通过 Jacobi theta 恒等式相联系。我们的主要贡献是一套统一的精确采样方案，适用于外推和插值，它 (i) 包含边界端点，并且 (ii) 自动在密度表示之间切换，以在不同情形下保持高效的接受率。为此，我们推导了简单的提议分布族，覆盖小时间和大时间区域，并给出自动选择更紧包络的规则。借助马尔可夫性质，我们的方法可扩展到对多个时刻的离散骨架路径进行精确模拟。

## Abstract
We study Brownian motion and Brownian bridge processes conditioned to remain in a fixed interval $[0,a]$, focusing on the conditional distribution at a single time. For a Brownian motion in $[0,a]$, conditioned on survival up to time $t$ we recall (and present in a self-contained form) the conditional density of its position at $t$ (exact extrapolation). For a Brownian bridge conditioned to remain in $[0,a]$ on $[0,T]$ we express the probability density at an interior time as a normalized product of killed transition densities (exact interpolation). These densities admit dual complementary series representations, which are linked via the Jacobi theta identity. Our main contribution is a unified exact sampling suite for both extrapolation and interpolation that (i) includes boundary endpoints and (ii) automatically switches between density representations to keep acceptance rates efficient across regimes. To that end, we derive simple proposal families which cover both small- and large-time regimes, with an automatic rule selecting the tighter envelope. Our procedures extend to exact simulation of discrete skeleton paths at multiple times via the Markov property.
