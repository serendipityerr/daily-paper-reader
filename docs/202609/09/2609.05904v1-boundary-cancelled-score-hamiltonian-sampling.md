## 摘要
扩散采样可以视为概率密度的虚时退火。从正向/反向欧几里得薛定谔对出发，我们表明固定加噪漂移会强制反向得分项出现，并且同一对数力是超对称得分哈密顿量的单侧虚时反绝热联络。这种对应关系将得分学习误差转化为哈密顿量微扰，并得出一个调度原则：若前r个终端导数消失，Morita–Nishimori边界抵消可将残余Hellinger误差从T^{-2}抑制到T^{-2r-2}，直至达到得分或采样的下限。反向SDE基准测试（包括二维学习得分测试）证实了所预测的改进。

## Abstract
Diffusion sampling can be viewed as imaginary-time annealing of probability densities. From a forward/backward Euclidean Schrödinger pair, we show that fixing the noising drift forces the reverse score term, and that the same logarithmic force is the one-sided imaginary-time counterdiabatic connection of a supersymmetric Score Hamiltonian. The correspondence turns score-learning error into a Hamiltonian perturbation and yields a schedule principle: if the first $r$ terminal derivatives vanish, Morita--Nishimori boundary cancellation suppresses the residual Hellinger error from $T^{-2}$ to $T^{-2r-2}$ until score or sampling floors are reached. Reverse-SDE benchmarks, including two-dimensional learned-score tests, confirm the predicted improvement.
