## 摘要
背景：金属植入物会在计算机断层成像（CT）中引起条状和阴影伪影，遮挡解剖结构并扭曲Hounsfield单位（HU）值。传统扩散模型从高斯噪声重建图像，而不是直接利用受伪影污染的CT中保留的解剖信息。目的：开发一种图像域金属伪影减少（MAR）方法，通过端点条件扩散桥连接受伪影污染的CT图像与无金属CT图像。方法：所提出的RDBM-MAR方法使用端点条件扩散桥，并从伪影CT图像初始化重建。条件U-Net从中间桥状态预测图像域校正。推理使用10个确定性反向步骤。我们使用了来自AAPM CT-MAR Grand Challenge的14,000个配对CT切片，包括12,045个训练、633个验证和1,322个留出测试切片。对一个临床基准病例进行了定性评估。我们使用PSNR、SSIM、MAE和LPIPS将RDBM-MAR与CycleGAN、Pix2Pix、Res-SRDiff、I2I-Mamba和I2SB进行比较。统计分析使用Friedman检验和经Holm校正的配对Wilcoxon符号秩检验。结果：RDBM-MAR取得了最高的PSNR（43.67 +/- 4.56 dB）和SSIM（0.9856 +/- 0.0138），以及最低的MAE（14.74 +/- 8.68 HU）。四种指标在各方法间均存在显著差异（p < 0.001）。RDBM-MAR在PSNR、SSIM和MAE上优于所有五个对照方法（校正后p < 0.001）；Res-SRDiff取得了最低的LPIPS。差异图、HU剖面和临床病例显示伪影减少，同时周围解剖结构得以保留。结论：RDBM-MAR提供了从伪影CT向无金属CT的直接恢复轨迹，使用10个反向步骤实现了较强的结构保真度和HU准确性。

## Abstract
Background: Metal implants cause streaking and shading artifacts in computed tomography (CT), obscuring anatomy and distorting Hounsfield-unit (HU) values. Conventional diffusion models reconstruct images from Gaussian noise rather than directly exploiting anatomical information retained in artifact-corrupted CT.   Purpose: To develop an image-domain metal artifact reduction (MAR) method connecting artifact-corrupted and metal-free CT images through an endpoint-conditioned diffusion bridge.   Methods: The proposed RDBM-MAR method uses an endpoint-conditioned diffusion bridge and initializes reconstruction from the artifact CT image. A conditional U-Net predicts an image-domain correction from intermediate bridge states. Inference uses 10 deterministic reverse steps. We used 14,000 paired CT slices from the AAPM CT-MAR Grand Challenge, including 12,045 training, 633 validation, and 1,322 held-out test slices. One clinical benchmark case was assessed qualitatively. We compared RDBM-MAR with CycleGAN, Pix2Pix, Res-SRDiff, I2I-Mamba, and I2SB using PSNR, SSIM, MAE, and LPIPS. Statistical analysis used Friedman and paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Holm correction.   Results: RDBM-MAR achieved the highest PSNR (43.67 +/- 4.56 dB) and SSIM (0.9856 +/- 0.0138) and the lowest MAE (14.74 +/- 8.68 HU). All four metrics differed significantly among methods (p < 0.001). RDBM-MAR outperformed all five comparators in PSNR, SSIM, and MAE (adjusted p < 0.001); Res-SRDiff achieved the lowest LPIPS. Difference maps, HU profiles, and the clinical case showed reduced artifacts with preservation of surrounding anatomy.   Conclusions: RDBM-MAR provides a direct restoration trajectory from artifact CT toward metal-free CT, achieving strong structural fidelity and HU accuracy using 10 reverse steps.
