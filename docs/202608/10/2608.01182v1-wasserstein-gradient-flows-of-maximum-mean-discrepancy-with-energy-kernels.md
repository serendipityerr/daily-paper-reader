## 摘要
我们研究了由非光滑能量核$K(z)=-|z|^q$（$0<q<2$）生成的平方最大平均差异（MMD）的Wasserstein梯度流。在维度$d\ge2$中，相应的能量不是位移半凸的，因此标准Wasserstein梯度流理论不适用。当$d+q-2>0$时，我们证明了在$\mathbb{R}^d$上，对于亚临界$L^p$空间中的概率密度，且目标属于同一可积性类并具有有限矩的情况下，全局适定性成立。我们还包含了一维Coulomb端点$d=q=1$。对于相关的$N$粒子系统，我们证明了全局非碰撞性和固定$N$下向无碰撞临界集的收敛性、粒子到连续统的临界性原理，以及一个调制能量平均场估计，该估计在$N\to\infty$时在每个有限时间区间上产生粒子动力学向连续统流的收敛。我们还构造了无碰撞鞍平衡，表明确定性粒子轨迹不一定接近全局经验最小化器。对于$1\le q<2$，我们类中的每个连续统解都具有窄相对紧轨道，每个$\omega$-极限点都是Lagrangian临界的，并且轨道接近Lagrangian临界集。对于$0<q<1$，在时间一致矩和亚临界$L^p$界下，同样的结论成立。我们证明了当源和目标具有$q$阶有限矩时，绝对连续的Lagrangian临界点等于目标，除非$0<q<1$且$d\in\{1,3\}$。在前述一致界下，刚性给出在适定性范围的刚性部分中连续统流向目标的收敛。最后，我们表明在$\mathbb{R}^d$上不存在与初始数据无关的乘性MMD衰减模，并且在几个全空间和周期Riesz/Coulomb区域中全局Polyak--Łojasiewicz不等式不成立。

## Abstract
We study the Wasserstein gradient flow of the squared Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD) generated by the nonsmooth energy kernels $K(z)=-|z|^q$, $0<q<2$. In dimensions $d\ge2$, the corresponding energies are not displacement semiconvex, so standard Wasserstein-gradient-flow theory does not apply. When $d+q-2>0$, we prove global well-posedness on $\mathbb{R}^d$ for probability densities in subcritical $L^p$ spaces, with targets in the same integrability class and with finite moments. We also include the one-dimensional Coulomb endpoint $d=q=1$.   For the associated $N$-particle system, we prove global noncollision and fixed-$N$ convergence to the collision-free critical set, a particle-to-continuum criticality principle, and a modulated-energy mean-field estimate that yields convergence of the particle dynamics to the continuum flow as $N\to\infty$ on every finite time interval. We also construct collision-free saddle equilibria, showing that deterministic particle trajectories need not approach global empirical minimizers.   For $1\le q<2$, every continuum solution in our class has a narrowly relatively compact orbit, every $ω$-limit point is Lagrangian critical, and the orbit approaches the Lagrangian critical set. For $0<q<1$, the same conclusions hold under uniform-in-time moment and subcritical $L^p$ bounds. We prove that an absolutely continuous Lagrangian critical point equals the target when the source and target have finite moments of order $q$, except when $0<q<1$ and $d\in\{1,3\}$. Under the preceding uniform bounds, rigidity gives convergence of the continuum flow to the target throughout the rigid part of the well-posedness range.   Finally, we show that no initial-data-independent multiplicative MMD decay modulus exists on $\mathbb{R}^d$, and that global Polyak--Łojasiewicz inequalities fail in several whole-space and periodic Riesz/Coulomb regimes.
