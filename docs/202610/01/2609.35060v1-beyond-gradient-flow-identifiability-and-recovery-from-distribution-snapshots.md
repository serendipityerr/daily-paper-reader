## 摘要
从演化分布的快照中推断动力学本质上是欠定的：Fokker-Planck 方程仅通过其 score 加权散度 ∇·F+F·∇logρ 约束漂移 F，从而留下一个 ρ-无散规范自由度，对任何单时刻约束都不可见。时间索引的输运表述无法解决这一歧义：每一条可容许的边缘分布路径都容许一种无旋解释，最小作用量重构会选择它，而仅靠边缘分布拟合无法区分动力学上不等价的解释。转而要求一个自治场解释多个边缘分布，会使部分隐藏环流变得可见，因为 ∇logρ 随边缘分布变化。将瞬时 Fokker-Planck 源约束与快照实验分离，我们证明源约束可在模去堆叠 score 加权散度算子之核的意义下识别该场。对于一般的高斯形状变化，在本征维数 m 下，K≥m 个时间点处的源约束可消除所有多项式规范方向，而仅有限多个密度快照则会出现混叠；我们明确给出这种障碍。在高斯锚点处，对于 Sobolev 光滑度 s 和每个时间点 n 个样本，我们推导出切空间快照实验的条件下界速率 (nK)^(-2s/(2s+m+1))，并在逐阶基准中给出匹配的上界速率。强形式拟合与 score 误差非正交，且无法通过谱滤波修复。相反，我们使用光滑检验函数进行估计，同时保留已知扩散项，并推导出一个有限样本界，将采样误差与固定网格求积偏差分离。植入环流实验证实了预测的规范收缩，并揭示了跨切片信息与协方差感知白化之间的设计张力。

## Abstract
Inferring dynamics from snapshots of evolving distributions is fundamentally underdetermined: the Fokker-Planck equation constrains the drift $F$ only through its score-weighted divergence $\nabla\cdot F+F\cdot\nabla\logρ$, leaving a $ρ$-solenoidal gauge invisible to any single-time constraint. Time-indexed transport formulations cannot resolve this ambiguity: every admissible marginal path admits a curl-free explanation, minimum-action reconstruction selects it, and marginal fit alone cannot distinguish dynamically inequivalent explanations. Requiring one autonomous field to explain several marginals instead makes part of the hidden circulation visible as $\nabla\logρ$ changes across marginals. Separating instantaneous Fokker-Planck source constraints from the snapshot experiment, we show that the source constraints identify the field modulo the kernel of a stacked score-weighted divergence operator. For generic Gaussian shape variation, source constraints at $K\ge m$ time points in intrinsic dimension $m$ eliminate every polynomial gauge direction, whereas finitely many density snapshots alone admit aliasing; we give the obstruction explicitly. At a Gaussian anchor, for Sobolev smoothness $s$ and $n$ samples per time point, we derive a conditional lower rate $(nK)^{-2s/(2s+m+1)}$ for the tangent snapshot experiment, with a matching upper rate in a degreewise benchmark. Strong-form fitting is non-orthogonal to score error and cannot be repaired by spectral filtering. Instead, we estimate using smooth test functions while retaining the known diffusion term, and derive a finite-sample bound that separates sampling error from fixed-grid quadrature bias. Planted-circulation experiments confirm the predicted gauge contraction and expose a design tension between cross-slice information and covariance-aware whitening.
